Methodology & caveats
  • Home Assistant entities queried Sept 20, 2026 (device-level entities Sept 10; Emporia Vue branch-circuit entities Sept 20). “This month” sensors reset on the calendar month, not NYSEG's ~10th-to-10th billing cycle, so early-month figures look small next to a full bill.
  • “Estimated” monthly kWh assumes a device's live wattage holds constant 24/7 — a reasonable approximation for always-on gear (UPS, network switch, repeaters), less so for anything on a schedule or photocell, and not used for the Emporia branch circuits, which are shown as live-only readings, not monthly estimates.
  • The $0.251/kWh blended rate used above is computed directly from 14 real NYSEG bill PDFs (electric cost ÷ kWh, each month), not a delivery-rate estimate. It ranged $0.2103/kWh (Sep ’26) to $0.3016/kWh (May ’26) as the variable supply rate moved — treat $60/mo as a central estimate, not exact.
