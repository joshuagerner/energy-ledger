Gaps & recommendations

info
New: Chest Freezer & Basement Refrigerator plug, added Sept 10

Renamed from “Chest Freezer” because one ZHA smart plug (switch.chest_freezer) actually meters a single downstream circuit covering three things: the chest freezer, the basement refrigerator, and a small PoE switch that powers the Shed Access Point and a Ubiquiti Bullet camera. None of the three can be metered separately — the sensor only sees their combined draw (17.7W live as of today). Neither the PoE switch, the Shed AP, nor the camera exist as their own HA entities. Once a full month of data accrues, a monthly kWh figure can be added here the same way the Garage Outlet and Shed repeater get theirs (30-day statistics delta) — there's no per-month reset sensor on this device, only a lifetime total that a “Reset total energy” button can zero out if you ever want a clean monthly cycle.

gap
Major loads have zero visibility

Nothing in Home Assistant currently monitors HVAC/heat, water heater, laundry, range/oven, or an EV charger if you have one. Nationally these typically make up 60–80% of a home's electricity use, and are almost certainly most of the ~84% currently unaccounted for above (electric-only comparison — see The Gap).

warning
105-gallon tank equipment isn’t power-monitored

Its heater, CO2 solenoid, filter pump, and WRGB light have no real wattage sensors — only the WLED accent strip's rough current estimate. Every other tank in the house is metered per-outlet; this one's the outlier.

warning
Driveway light sensor is offline

Both its power and energy entities report unavailable, so it's currently invisible rather than zero.

gap
Gas heat is likely your single biggest cost, and it's invisible here by design

Your NYSEG statement bills electricity and natural gas together. With 14 months on record, gas peaked at $287.83 in February (279 therms) and bottomed out around $51–$55/mo by late summer — a clean heating signature, over 5× winter-to-summer swing. No device on this page monitors gas, and none could: it's a separate delivery system entirely. Worth remembering when the “84% unaccounted” figure above looks alarming — some of that belongs to gas-adjacent electric loads (furnace blower, circulator pumps), but most of your winter bill swing is gas, not missing electric monitoring.

gap
Electricity itself peaks in summer, not winter — a second, separate load HA doesn't explain

Monthly electric usage swings too: 865–1,247 kWh in the shoulder/winter months vs. 1,720–1,730 kWh in July and August. That's a ~2× summer jump with no obvious source among the devices already tracked here (aquariums, office gear, and accent lighting don't swing seasonally). The likely culprit is cooling — central AC, window units, or a pool/well pump — and it's currently one of the largest unmonitored loads in the house, alongside gas heat in winter.

Methodology & caveats
  • Home Assistant entities queried Sept 10, 2026. “This month” sensors reset on the calendar month, not NYSEG's ~10th-to-10th billing cycle, so early-month figures look small next to a full bill.
  • “Estimated” monthly kWh assumes a device's live wattage holds constant 24/7 — a reasonable approximation for always-on gear (UPS, network switch, repeaters), less so for anything on a schedule or photocell.
  • The $0.251/kWh blended rate used above is computed directly from 14 real NYSEG bill PDFs (electric cost ÷ kWh, each month), not a delivery-rate estimate. It ranged $0.2103/kWh (Sep ’26) to $0.3016/kWh (May ’26) as the variable supply rate moved — treat $60/mo as a central estimate, not exact.
  • A Sept 10, 2026 entity audit turned up six Zigbee smart-plug sensors not previously on this page (Standup Freezer, Shed, Sump pump v.2, Hutch, Snowflakes, Movie room) — all verified as real device_class: power/energy sensors before being added, using 30-day statistics deltas rather than live-wattage guesses. The Standup Freezer was originally catalogued as “Garage Zigbee Repeater Outlet” until the homeowner confirmed its real identity later that same day — see “Utility, garage & seasonal plugs” and the resolved item under Gaps.
  • NYSEG bills combine electricity and natural gas on one statement. Every figure above that compares to “your bill” uses the electric-only line item, never the combined total, since gas isn't something any device here monitors.
  • Bill trend reflects 14 detailed bill PDFs you uploaded (Jul 2025–Sep 2026), plus a Green Button interval-data export (pulled Sept 2026) that filled in Aug 2025's kWh (1,454, matching the $399.34 total on the Sep 2025 bill). Aug 2025's electric/gas cost split is still unknown with no PDF for that month — the blended $0.251/kWh rate above is unaffected by this, since it's cost-weighted across the 14 PDF months only.
  • “Monthly avg” on each device card is pulled from real Home Assistant statistics, not a projection of the current MTD figure. Aquariums & Living room use the average of the two most recent complete calendar months (Jul–Aug ’26) — the Aquarium strip's earlier months (Mar–May) were excluded after a data discontinuity turned up in its stats history around June, likely from a device re-pairing. Exterior lighting averages every complete month on record per sensor (varies 6–13 months). Office & network reuses the existing 24/7 live-wattage estimate scaled to a full month. Utility/garage/seasonal keeps its existing last-30-day figure as the avg and adds a real Sept 1–10 MTD alongside it; the new Chest Freezer & Basement Refrigerator plug isn't in that avg yet — see its Gaps note. All dollar figures use the $0.2505/kWh 14-bill blended rate.
