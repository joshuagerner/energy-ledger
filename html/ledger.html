Methodology & caveats
  • Home Assistant entities queried Sept 20, 2026 (device-level entities Sept 10; Emporia Vue branch-circuit entities Sept 20). “This month” sensors reset on the calendar month, not NYSEG's ~10th-to-10th billing cycle, so early-month figures look small next to a full bill.
  • “Estimated” monthly kWh assumes a device's live wattage holds constant 24/7 — a reasonable approximation for always-on gear (UPS, network switch, repeaters), less so for anything on a schedule or photocell, and not used for the Emporia branch circuits, which are shown as live-only readings, not monthly estimates.
  • The $0.251/kWh blended rate used above is computed directly from 14 real NYSEG bill PDFs (electric cost ÷ kWh, each month), not a delivery-rate estimate. It ranged $0.2103/kWh (Sep ’26) to $0.3016/kWh (May ’26) as the variable supply rate moved — treat $60/mo as a central estimate, not exact.
  • A Sept 10, 2026 entity audit turned up six Zigbee smart-plug sensors not previously on this page (Standup Freezer, Shed, Sump pump v.2, Hutch, Snowflakes, Movie room) — all verified as real device_class: power/energy sensors before being added, using 30-day statistics deltas rather than live-wattage guesses. The Standup Freezer was originally catalogued as “Garage Zigbee Repeater Outlet” until the homeowner confirmed its real identity later that same day — see “Utility, garage & seasonal plugs” and the resolved item under Gaps.
  • New (Sept 17–20, 2026): an Emporia Vue whole-home energy monitor was installed, adding 3 mains legs (Mains_C unused) and 16 branch-circuit CT clamps, plus a synthetic “Balance” sensor for the unmonitored remainder. 11 of 16 circuits are confirmed as of this snapshot, always via a live toggle test or the homeowner's direct statement, never by wattage-coincidence guessing — several early guesses (a Circuit 13/freezer match, a Circuit 6/Standup-Freezer match, a Balance/second-AC-leg theory) were explicitly walked back once contradicted by cleaner evidence. Emporia's own CSV export numbers circuits 1–16; Home Assistant's entity numbering is offset by one (CSV Circuit N = HA Channel N+1) — this page follows the homeowner's own “Circuit N (HA Channel N+1)” notation throughout. Full identification history and the still-unidentified circuits are also tracked in the project's companion doc.
  • New (Sept 21, 2026): added the Whole-home cross-check card, tracking Emporia's Main sensor and NYSEG's billed kWh as two independent figures rather than merging them — the goal is to surface any discrepancy between the utility's own meter and Emporia's, not let either supersede the other.
